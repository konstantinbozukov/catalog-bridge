# VA playbook — Medium (v0)

**Adapter:** assisted / syndication

## Approach

Republish or adapt comp page content as a Medium article. Host domain already has some SEO juice; kitchen constraint articles fit the platform.

## Before publishing

- [ ] Article adds value beyond copy-paste (intro story, constraint framing, table)
- [ ] One primary catalog bridge CTA (link to full comp on our site)
- [ ] FTC disclosure at top or bottom
- [ ] Canonical: prefer linking **to our site** as the source of truth for picks/tables

## Article shape

1. Hook: the constraint problem (“studio kitchen, $100 cap, need an air fryer”)
2. How we chose (methodology summary)
3. Top 3 picks in prose
4. Link to full comparison table on catalog site
5. Disclosure

## Rate limits

- 1–2 articles per week on new account
- Don’t syndicate identical text to 10 platforms same day

## Logging

Medium article URL, comp slug, date → post log.
