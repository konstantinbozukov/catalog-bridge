# VA playbook — Pinterest (v0)

**Adapter:** assisted

## Account setup

1. Business or personal account with kitchen/home board theme
2. Create boards aligned to constraints: “Small Kitchen Appliances”, “Budget Coffee”, etc.
3. Pin 10+ non-affiliate inspiration pins before catalog pins

## Pin format

- Vertical image (1000×1500 or similar) — simple product collage or text overlay with constraint title
- Title: match search intent (“Best Air Fryer for Small Kitchen Under $100”)
- Description: 2–3 sentences + disclosure + **catalog bridge URL only**
- Link: comp page URL (never Amazon)

## Rate limits

- 3–5 new pins per day max on new account
- Spread across boards; don’t spam identical pin

## Logging

Record pin URL, comp slug, date in post log.

## Kill signals

- Pin rejected or account flagged → reduce volume, review image/text for spam patterns
